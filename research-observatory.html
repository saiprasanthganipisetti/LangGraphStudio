🔭 RESEARCH OBSERVATORY

Teach your graph to investigate

Research assistants gather evidence, inspect sources, identify uncertainty and produce useful summaries. In TeamPilot, this helps the team understand unfamiliar technologies without pretending to know what it has not verified.

Byte says: “A good explorer doesn't guess what's behind the mountain. They collect clues, check where each clue came from, and tell the team what is known, uncertain and worth testing!”

🧭 Research Assistant journey

1️⃣ Understand the question
Clarify what the team needs to decide.
2️⃣ Plan the investigation
Break the question into searchable sub-questions.
3️⃣ Gather evidence
Call approved search, docs or internal tools.
4️⃣ Verify sources
Check authority, recency, relevance and agreement.
5️⃣ Synthesise findings
Separate facts, assumptions and unknowns.
6️⃣ Recommend next steps
Propose a learning task, POC or decision.
A research agent should not turn search snippets into unquestioned truth. Preserve source metadata and let the application enforce which domains, tools and actions are permitted.

🧰 Tool calling is a loop

The model decides whether a tool is useful and proposes arguments. The application validates permissions and arguments, executes the tool, then returns the result to the model.

  1. Model requests a search.
  2. Runtime validates the tool call.
  3. Tool retrieves evidence.
  4. Result is added to the conversation.
  5. Model synthesises or asks for another tool.

🧪 Research quality checklist

  • Prefer official documentation for API and product behaviour.
  • Record URL, title, publisher and retrieval date.
  • Check publication/update dates for fast-changing technologies.
  • Use more than one independent source for consequential claims.
  • Label assumptions and confidence; don't fabricate citations.
  • Escalate when evidence is insufficient or contradictory.

🧱 Build a research report that decision-makers can trust




Finding
What the source explicitly supports.


Source
Where the claim came from and when it was checked.


Confidence
High, medium or low, with a reason.


Assumption
What the team is inferring rather than observing.


Open question
What remains unresolved.


Recommendation
Research more, run a POC, learn, collaborate or defer.




For TeamPilot, research should inform a human decision. It should not silently assign a technology owner, approve spend or modify production infrastructure.
